## 🚀 Overview

Foodie is a full-stack AI-powered food commerce application built to go beyond traditional food ordering.

Instead of forcing users to manually search through menus, Foodie allows them to express what they want in natural language. The AI interprets their intent, retrieves relevant dishes from the real restaurant catalog, and provides personalized recommendations.

The platform also introduces AI-assisted group meal planning, where multiple users can combine different preferences and optimize a shared food order within a defined budget.

For commerce actions, Foodie follows a simple principle:

> **AI can recommend and optimize — but money movement remains bounded, explainable, and user-controlled.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI Food Commerce Assistant

Users can describe their requirements naturally instead of manually filtering menus.

Example:

> "Find me European dishes under ₹1000"

The AI:
- Understands the user's intent
- Identifies relevant constraints
- Searches the real food catalog
- Retrieves matching menu items
- Generates contextual recommendations

This creates a conversational discovery experience instead of traditional menu browsing.

---

### 🧠 AI Group Meal Planner

Foodie makes group ordering easier by allowing the host to define preferences for each participant.

Each member can specify:

- Dietary preference
- Spice level
- Craving
- Personal preferences
- Group budget

The AI then generates a meal combination that attempts to satisfy the group while remaining within the defined budget.

Example:

```text
Group Budget: ₹3000

Member 1:
Diet: Non-Veg
Spice: Spicy
Craving: Chicken

Member 2:
Diet: Non-Veg
Spice: Medium
Craving: Lamb

Recommended Meal: ₹1930
Remaining Budget: ₹1070
